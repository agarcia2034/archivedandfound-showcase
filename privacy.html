Privacy Policy — archivedandfound

Last updated: 2026-11-13

archivedandfound operates this public catalog showcase to display curated vintage fashion, fine art, jewelry, and collectibles available on eBay.

Data Collection & Cookies

This static showcase site does not require user accounts, forms, or direct checkout, and does not collect or sell personal information. Clicking product links directs visitors to eBay.com or Pinterest.com, which are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Pinterest Integration

Our catalog syndicates public product imagery and descriptions to our official Pinterest business boards via RSS and the Pinterest API strictly to help collectors discover active listings.
